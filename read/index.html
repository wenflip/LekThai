The spellings and the new sign words haven’t been checked by a native Thai speaker yet, and the audio is your phone’s built-in voice. If the voice and the letters disagree, trust the letters here and tell us.

How to use it

① Hear & Choose plays a letter, vowel, number or word. Tap the Thai that matches. Wrong choices are look-alikes on purpose: telling them apart is the skill.

② See & Say shows Thai only. Say it out loud first, then tap reveal to hear it and check yourself. Be honest: if you hesitated, tap Missed.

Letters play their full name, like a picture alphabet: ด is “dor dek” (เด็ก, child). Several letters share one sound, and the picture word is what tells them apart.

Vowels are shown on อ, a silent letter that holds a vowel when there’s no consonant. Some vowels are written before the consonant but said after it, like เ in เด็ก.

Start with Letters 1 and Vowels 1, then the words you already know from /words. Reading words you can already say is the fastest way in.
