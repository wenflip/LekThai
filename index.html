How it works

👂

Hear it

Listen first, then work out what you heard. Instant feedback, then the next one.

🗣️

Say it

Say it aloud from memory, then tap to hear the Thai and check yourself.

🎯

Weak spots, first

What you miss comes back sooner and more often. What you know fades to quick check-ins.

Why it works

LekThai is built on retrieval practice and spaced repetition — the same principles behind flashcard tools like Anki. Every prompt makes you pull the answer from memory before you see it, and that effort is what makes it stick. A few focused minutes a day beats one long cram.
