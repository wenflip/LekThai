How it works

👁️

See & Say

A number appears. Say it aloud, then tap to hear the Thai and check yourself.

🔊

Hear & Type

Listen to a Thai number and type the digits. Instant feedback, then the next one.

🎯

Weak spots, first

Numbers you miss come back sooner and more often. Mastered ones fade to quick check-ins.

Why it works

LekThai is built on retrieval practice and spaced repetition — the same principles behind flashcard tools like Anki. Thai numbers are a small, regular system, not thousands of separate facts, so a few focused minutes a day is enough to make them automatic.
