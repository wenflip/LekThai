How the course works

12 stages, in order. Each stage teaches a few new letters, then words made only from letters you already know.

Three parts per stage: ① Letters, ② Words, ③ Spell. Each part is practice first (not marked), then a short check.

Pass a check with 90% to get a tick: 18/20 for letters, 9/10 for words and spelling. Three ticks and you’re ready for the next stage.

Not yet? Tap “Practise these” to drill only what you missed, then try the check again. Unlimited tries. Every stage stays open, so you can jump around whenever you like.

Tones are never tested. The voice always says words with the right tone, so copy it.
