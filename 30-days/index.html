How it works

Every day has two 15-minute sessions. ① Listen trains your ear: numbers, words and phrases you hear and pick. ② Speak makes you say them out loud from memory, then check yourself.

Morning and night works best, but any time is fine. Missed a day? Just carry on. Want to skip ahead? Tap any day.

New things come back 1, 3, 7 and 14 days after you first meet them, and anything you miss comes back sooner.

★ Tests on Days 7, 14, 21 and 30 are optional. Pass with 80% overall and enough spoken right. Retake as often as you like.

Pausing

If you don’t tap anything for 5 seconds the clock pauses (10 seconds in Speak, so you have time to say it out loud). A pause is never a wrong answer. Tap to carry on.

Be honest in Speak

Speak is self-rated. If you hesitated, or it didn’t sound like what you said when you heard it back, tap Missed. It comes back sooner, which is the point.
